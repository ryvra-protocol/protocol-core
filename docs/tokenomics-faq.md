# Tokenomics FAQ (Draft)

## 1) What are PoT points?

PoT points are pre-TGE program units used to track eligible contribution activity in Ryvra.

## 2) Are points tokens?

No. Points are not tokens and do not imply market value, ownership rights, or guaranteed conversion.

## 3) How are points capped?

The initial cap is 2 points per account per day, with proportional halving by epoch under the draft framework.

## 4) Can abuse affect points?

Yes. Sybil behavior, wash patterns, and other abuse signals can reduce or invalidate points.

## 5) Is TGE conversion guaranteed?

No. Any future conversion framework is conditional and subject to legal review, compliance constraints, and governance decisions.

## 6) Why use a points model pre-TGE?

It enables contribution tracking and policy iteration before finalizing long-term token mechanics.

## 7) Where will updates be published?

Through Ryvra RFCs and official protocol documentation updates.
